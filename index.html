Is this free to use?

Can I save a PDF?

Click Print / PDF and select Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog.

Does the writing helper use live AI?

No. The current wording helper uses simple local text rules, not a live AI model.

Is my personal information uploaded?

No. This page processes your document details in your browser and does not send them to a LOXT server.
